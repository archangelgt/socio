AI moderation

The Platform may analyze comments for signals such as:

5. Automatic Comment Moderation

When a social network sends a comment to the Platform:

  1. The event is authenticated and validated.
  2. The comment is stored according to the customer's configuration.
  3. The moderation engine analyzes the content.
  4. The result is evaluated against the customer's moderation policy.
  5. Permissions and channel capabilities are checked.
  6. If authorized, the configured action may be sent to the social network.
  7. The action is recorded in an audit trail.

Where configured, the Platform may automatically hide comments that meet the organization's moderation rules.

AI moderation can produce false positives and false negatives. Customers remain responsible for configuring appropriate policies and review processes.

6. Human Review

Organizations may configure uncertain or high-risk cases for human review.

Moderators may be able to:

  • allow a comment;
  • hide a comment;
  • delete a comment where supported;
  • escalate;
  • mark a false positive;
  • restore a previously hidden comment where supported.
