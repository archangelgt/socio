User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Socio, a product of Seraph Systems, S.A., provides this page so you can request deletion of personal data we hold, including data received through Meta (Facebook and Instagram) integrations.

Use this URL in the Meta App Dashboard as the User data deletion instructions URL:

https://socio.seraphsystems.com/data-deletion

These instructions apply to everyone whose data Socio may process, not only Socio account holders. Related documents: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service.

1. Who Can Request Deletion

You may request deletion if you are:

  • a Socio user or organization owner with a workspace at https://socio.seraphsystems.com;
  • a Facebook or Instagram user who authorized the Socio Meta app;
  • a person whose comments, messages, profile name, or other content were ingested because a Page or Instagram account you interacted with is connected to Socio.

2. How Socio Account Holders Request Deletion

  1. Sign in at https://socio.seraphsystems.com if you still have access.
  2. Email privacy@seraphsystems.com from the email address on your Socio account.
  3. Use the subject line Socio data deletion request.
  4. Include your full name, Socio email, organization/workspace name, and the Facebook Page or Instagram account names you connected, if you know them.
  5. Say whether you want (a) deletion of a specific connected social account, (b) deletion of your user account, or (c) deletion of the entire organization workspace.

Organization owners may request deletion of the workspace and associated social data. Individual members may request deletion of their Socio user profile; organization content owned by the workspace is deleted only if an owner requests it or as required by law.

3. How Facebook and Instagram Users Request Deletion

If you used Facebook Login or granted Socio access to a Page or Instagram account, or if your public comments or messages were processed after a Page connected Socio, you can request deletion without a Socio login.

  1. Email privacy@seraphsystems.com.
  2. Use the subject line Meta user data deletion request.
  3. Include as much of the following as you can: your Facebook or Instagram name; your Facebook or Instagram user ID if you have it; the Page or account you commented on or messaged; approximate dates; and a copy or description of the comment or message.
  4. We will search our systems for matching records and delete eligible personal data associated with you.

4. How to Remove Socio from Facebook

You can also remove the app from your Facebook account. That stops future authorization from that Facebook user. You should still email us so we can delete stored tokens and related records.

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Apps and websites.
  4. Find Socio (or Seraph Systems / socio.seraphsystems.com) in your logged-in apps.
  5. Select Remove.
  6. Email privacy@seraphsystems.com with the subject Meta user data deletion request so we can complete deletion on our side.

Removing the app on Facebook does not by itself delete copies already stored in Socio. The email request is required for deletion from our systems.

5. What We Delete

When a request is verified, we delete or anonymize eligible data, which may include:

  • Socio account name, email, and authentication records;
  • organization membership and workspace settings, if requested by an owner;
  • connected Facebook Page and Instagram identifiers and profile names;
  • OAuth access tokens and related credentials;
  • stored comments, messages, posts, and attachments;
  • AI classifications, confidence scores, and moderation queue items;
  • audit records that are not legally required to be kept;
  • session records tied to the deleted user.

6. What We May Retain

Deletion may not immediately remove:

  • records we must keep under applicable law;
  • security, fraud-prevention, and abuse logs;
  • billing or accounting records, if billing was used;
  • information needed to resolve disputes or enforce agreements;
  • encrypted backups, until they expire or are rotated in the ordinary backup cycle;
  • information held by Facebook, Instagram, or other third parties on their own platforms.

Deleting information from Socio does not delete it from Meta. To delete content on Facebook or Instagram, use those products' own tools.

7. Timeframe and Status

We aim to acknowledge requests within seven (7) days and to complete eligible deletion within thirty (30) days of verification, unless a longer period is required by law or by the complexity of the request.

We may ask you to verify your identity or authority before we delete data. If we cannot locate matching records, we will tell you.

To check the status of a request, reply to our confirmation email or write to privacy@seraphsystems.com with your original subject line and any confirmation we sent you.
